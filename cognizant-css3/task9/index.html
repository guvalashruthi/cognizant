Community Bulletin

The city council has announced several new community development programs aimed at improving public spaces and encouraging local participation in cultural events. Residents are invited to join upcoming workshops, environmental awareness drives, and recreational activities planned throughout the month.

In addition, local artists and small businesses will be featured during the annual community fair scheduled for next weekend. The event will include live music performances, food stalls, exhibitions, and educational sessions for students and families.

Officials also emphasized the importance of sustainable living and encouraged citizens to take part in tree plantation initiatives and neighborhood clean-up campaigns. Volunteers can register through the city community portal.
